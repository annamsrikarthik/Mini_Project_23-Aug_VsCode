# Campaign context — discovery emails

This is a classroom Salesforce CRM outreach exercise, not a verified Salesforce
edition feature catalogue. We have not established the trial's exact edition.

## Purpose

Acknowledge a recorded demo request when the source says so. For other sources,
open a neutral conversation about the lead's needs. Ask how the team manages
customer relationships and invite a short discovery conversation.

## Writing rules

- Friendly, professional, concise; use known company or contact details.
- A demo request does not mean a demo took place.
- Do not assume a need, meeting, purchase intention, or specific challenge.
- Do not invent missing fields, sender identity, scheduling links, or customer stories.
- Do not mention specific edition features, prices, discounts, trial duration,
  support entitlements, implementation commitments, or guaranteed outcomes.
- Use no claims of Salesforce employment or partnership.
- Save the email as an unsent draft for sales review.

Add product claims only after verifying the exact offering and recording a source.
