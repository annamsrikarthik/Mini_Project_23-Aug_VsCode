## Human-review outcome

- Recorded lead facts were retained.
- Missing information was not invented.
- Demo wording was used only for the recorded demo request.
- Unsupported performance promises were removed.
- Each email contains one simple next step.
- Bulk sending remains outside the assignment scope.
- The controlled Gmail test reaching Spam shows that content quality and email deliverability are separate concerns. A production rollout should verify SPF, DKIM, DMARC, sender reputation, consent, and unsubscribe handling.
